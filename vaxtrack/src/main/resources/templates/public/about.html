ABOUT US

Built for a Healthier Nation

VaxTrack is the Ministry of Health's national platform for managing vaccination appointments, records, and stock — end to end, digitally.

Our Mission

Vaccination programs run smoothly when everyone involved — citizens, hospital staff, district coordinators, and national health authorities — has clear, real-time information. VaxTrack replaces paper-based booking and scattered record-keeping with one connected system, so a dose given at a district hospital is instantly reflected in the national record.

Who We Serve

From citizens booking their next dose, to nurses verifying appointments at the point of care, to the Ministry of Health tracking national stock — VaxTrack is built around the real workflow of a public vaccination program, not a generic booking form.

What We Stand For

🛡Trust

Every record is handled under strict data protection standards.

⚡Simplicity

Booking a dose should take minutes, not a trip to a government office.

🌐Access

Available to every citizen, in every district, on any device.

🔒Integrity

Historical records are never lost — only ever corrected transparently.
